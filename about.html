About BrightSpark

A local electrical team you can rely on

BrightSpark Electrical Services is a Johannesburg-based electrical services company established in 2021. We provide electrical solutions to homeowners, landlords and small businesses across Gauteng.

Our work is completed by qualified electricians and is quoted before the work starts.
